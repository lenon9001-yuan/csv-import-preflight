About

A clearer CSV before your next import.

Made by Lenon

I am Lenon, the independent maker of CSV Import Preflight, based in Malaysia. I am building a tool for people who prepare contact lists for CRMs and business applications.

Why this tool exists

Duplicate emails, inconsistent dates, and missing required values can interrupt an import. This tool helps you check the file and review proposed edits before you use the results.

What you can use today

The free browser tool checks required fields, email formats, phone numbers, and dates. It separates exact duplicates and offers optional cleanup for names, company spacing, recognized country values, and address spacing.

Four files to review

Download clean.csv, rejected.csv, duplicates.csv, and full-report.csv. The full report records the status, issues, and automatic changes for every source row.

Current scope

The beta handles one CSV at a time, up to 20 MB. We recommend 50,000 rows or fewer. PDF conversion is planned. Feedback on real import workflows helps guide development.

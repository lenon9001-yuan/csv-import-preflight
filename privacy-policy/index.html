Privacy Policy

How your information is handled.

Effective 22 November 2026

Operator

Lenon operates CSV Import Preflight from Malaysia. This policy covers the website, browser tool, and support correspondence.

CSV processing

Your selected CSV is read and processed in your browser tab. CSV contents, file names, and generated reports are not uploaded to our application server. The application does not save CSV records in browser local storage or a customer database. Closing or reloading the tab clears its in-memory results. Downloaded files stay on your device until you remove them.

Hosting and request information

Vercel hosts this website. Website delivery involves technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, requested URLs, and request times. Vercel may process this information for delivery, diagnostics, and security under its own policies.

Fonts

Pages request fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser shares technical request information with Google when fetching these resources. Font requests do not include CSV contents.

Cookies and analytics

The current application does not set application cookies or install advertising and analytics tracking tags. Hosting infrastructure may process technical information as described above. If advertising or tracking is introduced, this policy and any required user choices will be updated.

Support correspondence

When you email support, your email address, message, and any attachments you send are handled through the email provider to answer your request. Use fictional or redacted examples instead of customer contact lists. Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve your request, related disputes, and required recordkeeping. Providers manage their own technical retention periods and may process information outside Malaysia.

Payments

The current public website offers a free tool. If you separately agree to a paid service and follow a Stripe payment link, Stripe processes payment and billing information under its privacy policy. Details of any paid service will be provided before payment.

Your requests

Contact the email below to ask about support data, request access or correction, or request deletion. Applicable law determines your rights and any records that must be retained. CSV files processed only on your device cannot be retrieved by the operator.

Changes

Policy updates will appear here with a revised effective date.

Provider information is available in Vercel’s privacy policy, Google Fonts’ privacy information, and Stripe’s privacy policy.
